# Tiny CPU loop: results

Randomly initialised byte-level transformers trained on CPU on the procedural bank (`data/generated/bank`, seed 1). These prove the pipeline, not model quality: the tiny teacher stands in for Gemma 4 31B and the students for Gemma 4 E2B. Test split, calibrated with per-primitive temperatures fitted on val. Generated by `scripts/run_tiny_loop.sh`; numbers are copied from each `eval/report.json`.

## Reading these numbers

At this compute (2,500 steps x 32 items, about one epoch, 1-2M parameters, CPU) the tiny models learn priors and a few easy tasks (see each `eval/report.md` by task) but Choice stays near chance. This is under-training, not a pipeline bug: the same model overfits 64 Choice items to 100% batch accuracy in 100 steps (`runs/tiny/overfit_check.txt`). Calibration works as intended: calibrated ECE is 0.02-0.03 even where accuracy is low. The KD and log-loss-only students are within noise of each other because the tiny teacher is barely better than chance; distillation becomes meaningful with Gemma 4 31B as the teacher.

Teacher-score job: 30000 train items scored through the fake vLLM server, 30000 with label coverage >= 0.5.
